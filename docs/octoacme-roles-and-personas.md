# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers support engineering teams by aligning capacity and staffing with delivery needs, coaching team members, and escalating delivery risks.

### Responsibilities
- Plan team capacity and align technical staffing with project priorities
- Coach Developers and support their professional growth
- Identify delivery risks and remove or escalate execution blockers
- Partner on scope and staffing trade-offs

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- Work with Project Managers to assess capacity, timelines, dependencies, and delivery risks
- Partner with Product Managers on scope trade-offs and the engineering effort needed to meet product goals
- Support Developers by addressing execution blockers and providing coaching
- Coordinate with Technical Leads on technical staffing needs and delivery risks

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction and design decisions so solutions meet functional and non-functional requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Set technical direction and make or facilitate architecture decisions
- Define and review non-functional requirements such as scalability, reliability, and security
- Maintain the design and code review quality bar
- Collaborate on implementation patterns and test strategy

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- Guide Developers on implementation patterns and review designs and code
- Work with Product Managers to assess feasibility, constraints, and technical trade-offs
- Advise Project Managers on technical dependencies, estimates, and risks that may affect plans
- Coordinate with QA on test strategy and with DevOps / SRE on operational requirements

---

## UX Designer / Researcher

### Role Summary
UX Designers and Researchers shape usable, accessible experiences by translating user needs into validated flows and design guidance.

### Responsibilities
- Create user flows and wireframes to communicate proposed experiences
- Validate usability through research and testing
- Incorporate accessibility considerations into designs
- Synthesize user insights to inform product decisions

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to refine requirements using user research and usability findings
- Collaborate with Developers on design feasibility and implementation details
- Define UX acceptance criteria with QA so the experience can be validated
- Keep Project Managers informed of research, design dependencies, and validation milestones

---

## DevOps / SRE

### Role Summary
DevOps and Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) practitioners help teams release and operate services reliably, safely, and observably.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain reliable CI/CD pipelines and deployment safeguards
- Establish observability and incident readiness
- Prepare and validate rollback approaches
- Support operational response and service reliability

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with Developers and Technical Leads on deployment readiness, safeguards, and operational requirements
- Align release milestones and operational dependencies with Project Managers
- Advise Product Managers on reliability risks and release constraints that affect customer outcomes
- Coordinate incident communications with relevant stakeholders and work with Security / Compliance Representatives on secure delivery gates

---

## Security / Compliance Representative

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Representatives help teams identify risks and meet applicable security controls and compliance requirements throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Review threats and security risks and recommend mitigations
- Define and check security controls and compliance checkpoints
- Provide guidance for security incident escalation
- Help teams incorporate policy-driven requirements into delivery

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- Partner with Technical Leads and DevOps / SRE on secure design, delivery gates, and operational controls
- Work with Project Managers to record security and compliance risks and track checkpoints
- Collaborate with Product Managers to clarify policy-driven product requirements and trade-offs
- Advise Developers on security controls and support remediation of identified risks

---

## Customer Support / Success Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Success Liaisons bring customer-impact information into delivery and help teams prepare for and learn from releases.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer-impact trends and validate known issues
- Contribute to release readiness messaging
- Bring post-release feedback into the product feedback loop
- Provide reproducible issue reports and customer context

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to share customer feedback and inform prioritization
- Coordinate stakeholder updates and release communications with Project Managers
- Partner with Developers and QA to provide customer context and reproducible issue reports
- Share customer-impact trends with the wider delivery team during release readiness and post-release reviews
